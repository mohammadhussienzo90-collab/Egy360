## Phase 4: Growth Strategies (Ongoing)

### Organic Growth Tactics

#### Hashtag Strategy (Instagram)

Use 20-25 hashtags per post, mix of:
- 5 high-volume (1M+ posts): #egypt #travel #wanderlust
- 10 medium (100K-1M): #egypttravel #cairoegypt #pyramidsofgiza
- 10 niche (10K-100K): #egypttravelguide #visitegypt2025 #nilecruise

**Rotate these sets (from SOCIAL_MEDIA_GUIDE.md):**
- Set 1: Pyramids/Cairo focused
- Set 2: Luxor/Temples focused
- Set 3: Beach/Red Sea focused
- Set 4: General travel

#### Engagement Strategy

**Daily Engagement (Critical for growth):**
1. Search hashtags: #egypttravel #planningegypttrip
2. Comment meaningfully on 20-30 posts (not "Nice!" but real value)
3. Like posts from potential followers
4. Follow relevant accounts (unfollowed later if no followback)

**Example Valuable Comments:**
```
"The lighting in this shot is incredible! Did you visit during sunrise? That's definitely the best time for the Pyramids 🏛️"

"I love Luxor! The Karnak Temple at sunset is magical. Did you make it to the Valley of the Kings?"

"Egypt is on my bucket list! How many days do you recommend for first-timers?"
```

#### Collaboration Opportunities

- [ ] Identify 10 Egypt travel micro-influencers (5K-50K followers)
- [ ] Reach out for content swaps or shoutouts
- [ ] Comment regularly on their posts before pitching

**DM Template for Collaboration:**
```
Hey [Name]! 👋

Love your Egypt content - your [specific post] was amazing!

I run an Egypt travel guide (360egy.com) and would love to collaborate. We could do:
- Shoutout exchange
- I feature your content (with credit)
- Guest post swap

Let me know if you're interested! 🇪🇬
```

### Facebook-Specific Growth

#### Facebook Groups Strategy

**Join and engage in these groups:**
- Egypt Travel Tips
- Egypt Tourism & Travel
- Planning a Trip to Egypt
- Middle East Travel
- Family Travel Groups
- Budget Travel Groups

**Strategy:**
1. Join groups (5-10 relevant ones)
2. Be genuinely helpful - answer questions without being promotional

## Phase 6: Monetization Through Social

### Traffic Funnel

```
Instagram/Facebook Post
        ↓
    Link in Bio
        ↓
    360egy.com Blog
        ↓
  Affiliate Links (Tours/Hotels)
        ↓
      Commission
```

### Calls-to-Action (CTAs)

**Every post should have a CTA:**
- "Save this for your trip 🔖"
- "Link in bio for full guide"
- "Comment your questions 👇"
- "Share with your travel buddy"
- "Follow for daily Egypt tips"

### UTM Tracking

**Always use UTM parameters to track what's working:**
```
https://360egy.com/blog/pyramids-guide/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=pyramids_reel_jan
```

**Track in Google Analytics:**
- Acquisition → Traffic Acquisition → Filter by source/medium
- Set up Goals for affiliate clicks
