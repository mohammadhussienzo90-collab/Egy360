## 📞 FINANCIAL BEST PRACTICES

### Cash Flow Management
1. **Separate accounts**
   - Business account
   - Personal account
   - Emergency fund

2. **Track EVERYTHING**
   - Use accounting software (Wave - free)
   - Keep all receipts
   - Monthly reconciliation

3. **Pay yourself**
   - Set aside salary
   - Don't mix personal/business

### Payment Gateway Setup
- Stripe payouts: T+2 days
- Keep buffer for refunds: 10% of revenue
- Monitor transaction fees monthly

### Tax Planning (Egypt)
- VAT registration: When revenue >500,000 EGP/year
- Income tax: Progressive rates
- Keep receipts for deductions
- Quarterly tax estimates
