## MEDIUM 4: Scam Awareness
```
EGYPT SCAM ALERT: How to Avoid Tourist Traps 🚨

Egypt is safe, but scams exist. Here's how to stay smart:

❌ THE "FREE" GIFT SCAM
Someone gives you a "gift" then demands payment.
✅ Solution: Politely refuse anything "free"

❌ THE PHOTO SCAM
Someone offers to take your photo, then demands money.
✅ Solution: Take your own photos or agree on price FIRST

❌ THE "CLOSED" ATTRACTION
"The museum is closed, but I know a special entrance..."
✅ Solution: It's not closed. Walk past them.

❌ INFLATED TAXI PRICES
Drivers quoting crazy prices to tourists.
✅ Solution: Use Uber or agree on price BEFORE getting in

❌ THE HELPFUL "GUIDE"
Random person offers to show you around, then demands payment.
✅ Solution: Book official guides through your hotel or tour company

❌ FAKE PAPYRUS
"Genuine papyrus" that's actually banana leaf.
✅ Solution: Buy from reputable shops only

REMEMBER:
• If it feels wrong, walk away
• Always agree on prices beforehand
• "La shukran" = No thank you
• Most Egyptians are genuinely helpful!

Don't let fear stop you - just be aware! 💪

#egyptscams #travelsafety #egypttips #touristtraps #travelsmart
```

• Less rushed experience

💰 Entry: ~$20 USD

📸 Photos allowed (no flash inside)

• Bring water and snacks

## LONG 4: Money & Budget Guide (8 slides)
```
SLIDE 1 (Cover):
EGYPT MONEY GUIDE
Currency, Costs & Budgets
Save money on your trip 💰

---

SLIDE 2:
CURRENCY BASICS

Egyptian Pound (EGP / LE / £E)

Current rate (approximate):
$1 USD = ~30-50 EGP
€1 EUR = ~35-55 EGP

Notes: 1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200
Coins: Rarely used

ALWAYS check current rates before traveling!

---

SLIDE 3:
CASH vs CARDS

💵 CASH IS KING
• Many places don't accept cards
• Better for bargaining
• Tips always in cash
• Small vendors = cash only

💳 CARDS WORK AT:
• Hotels
• Large restaurants
• Major attractions
• Shopping malls

RECOMMENDATION:
Bring $200-300 USD
Withdraw EGP from ATMs as needed

---

SLIDE 4:
ATMs & EXCHANGE

🏧 ATMs
• Available everywhere in cities
• Use bank ATMs (safer)
• Withdraw in EGP
• Daily limits may apply
• Notify your bank before travel!

💱 EXCHANGE
• Airport rates are bad
• Hotels = convenient but poor rates
• Banks = best official rates
• Street changers = risky, avoid

PRO TIP: Compare rates before exchanging large amounts

---

SLIDE 5:
DAILY BUDGET GUIDE

🎒 BACKPACKER: $30-50/day
• Hostel: $10-15
• Street food: $5-10
• Transport: $5
• One activity: $15-20

💼 MID-RANGE: $80-150/day
• 3-star hotel: $40-60
• Restaurants: $20-30
• Tours: $30-50
• Transport: $10-15

💎 LUXURY: $250+/day
• 5-star hotel: $150+
• Fine dining: $50+
• Private tours: $100+
• Private transport

---

SLIDE 6:
WHAT THINGS COST

🍽️ FOOD
• Street food meal: $1-3
• Local restaurant: $5-10
• Nice restaurant: $15-30
• Hotel restaurant: $25-50

🚕 TRANSPORT
• Uber across Cairo: $3-8
• Metro ticket: $0.30
• Taxi (negotiated): $5-15

🎫 ATTRACTIONS
• Pyramids entry: $15
• Egyptian Museum: $10
• Valley of Kings: $10
• Most temples: $5-15

---

SLIDE 7:
TIPPING GUIDE (BAKSHEESH)

Tipping is EXPECTED in Egypt!

💵 RESTAURANTS
10-15% (if not included)

💵 TOUR GUIDES
$10-20 per day

💵 DRIVERS
$5-10 per day

💵 HOTEL STAFF
$1-2 per service

💵 BATHROOM ATTENDANTS
5-10 EGP ($0.20)

💵 SMALL FAVORS
10-20 EGP

Keep small bills ready!

---

SLIDE 8:
MONEY-SAVING TIPS

1️⃣ Eat like locals
Street food is cheap AND delicious

2️⃣ Book tours online
30-50% cheaper than on-site

3️⃣ Use Uber
Cheaper than negotiating taxis

4️⃣ Visit off-season
May-September = 30% cheaper

5️⃣ Haggle everything
Start at 50% of asking price

6️⃣ Stay in local areas
Giza > Downtown for Pyramids

7️⃣ Combo tickets
Some sites offer bundles

8️⃣ Free activities
Walking, sunsets, markets!

## LONG 5: Temple Guide (9 slides)
```
SLIDE 1 (Cover):
EGYPT'S MOST INCREDIBLE TEMPLES
A Complete Guide
Which ones to visit 🏛️

---

SLIDE 2:
KARNAK TEMPLE (Luxor)
⭐ MUST VISIT

Why it's special:
• LARGEST ancient religious site ever
• 2,000 years to build
• 134 massive columns
• Overwhelming in the best way

Time needed: 2-3 hours
Entry: ~$15 USD
Best time: Morning or late afternoon

Don't miss: The Great Hypostyle Hall

---

SLIDE 3:
LUXOR TEMPLE
⭐ MUST VISIT

Why it's special:
• RIGHT in Luxor city center
• Stunning at night (lit up!)
• Connected to Karnak by sphinx avenue
• Active for 3,000+ years

Time needed: 1-2 hours
Entry: ~$10 USD
Best time: SUNSET (magical lighting)

Don't miss: The illuminated night visit!

---

SLIDE 4:
ABU SIMBEL
⭐ MUST VISIT

Why it's special:
• Most impressive temple ever
• 4 giant Ramses II statues
• Was MOVED to save from flooding
• Sun illuminates inner chamber 2x/year

Time needed: 2 hours (+ travel)
Entry: ~$20 USD
Getting there: Day trip from Aswan

Don't miss: Being there at sunrise

---

SLIDE 5:
PHILAE TEMPLE (Aswan)
⭐ HIGHLY RECOMMENDED

Why it's special:
• Built on an ISLAND
• Dedicated to goddess Isis
• Beautiful boat ride to reach it
• Romantic atmosphere

Time needed: 2 hours
Entry: ~$10 USD
Best time: Late afternoon

Don't miss: The Sound & Light show

---

SLIDE 6:
HATSHEPSUT TEMPLE (Luxor)
⭐ HIGHLY RECOMMENDED

Why it's special:
• Built by a FEMALE pharaoh
• Carved into cliff face
• Unique architecture
• 3,500 years old

Time needed: 1-1.5 hours
Entry: Included in Valley ticket
Best time: Early morning (less hot)

Don't miss: The colonnaded terraces

---

SLIDE 7:
EDFU & KOM OMBO
⭐ GREAT IF ON NILE CRUISE

EDFU TEMPLE:
• Best preserved temple in Egypt
• Dedicated to Horus (falcon god)
• Reached by horse carriage

KOM OMBO TEMPLE:
• Unusual double temple
• Half for Sobek (crocodile god)
• Mummified crocodiles on display!

Usually visited during Nile cruise

---

SLIDE 8:
MORE TEMPLES TO KNOW

DENDERA
• Stunning ceiling art
• Zodiac ceiling
• Less touristy

ABYDOS
• Beautiful reliefs
• Important pilgrimage site
• Off the beaten path

MEDINET HABU
• Mortuary temple
• Colorful reliefs
• Quiet and underrated

ESNA
• Small but beautiful
• Quick stop on Nile cruise

---

SLIDE 9:
TEMPLE VISITING TIPS

👗 DRESS CODE
Cover shoulders & knees
Loose, breathable clothes

☀️ GO EARLY
Temples are HOT midday
Fewer crowds at opening

📖 GET A GUIDE
Stories make it 10x better
$20-40 for a few hours

📸 PHOTOS ALLOWED
Usually free now
No flash inside

🦯 COMFORTABLE SHOES
Lots of walking
Uneven surfaces

💧 BRING WATER
Stay hydrated!
