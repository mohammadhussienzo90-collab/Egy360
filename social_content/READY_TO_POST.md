## POST 2: Pyramids Must-Know Tips (Carousel or Single)

### Caption:
```
5 things you NEED to know before visiting the Pyramids 🏛️

Trust me, I wish someone told me this before my first visit!

1️⃣ GO EARLY OR LATE
Skip the midday crowds and heat. Sunrise (8am) or late afternoon (3-4pm) = perfect lighting + fewer tourists

2️⃣ BOOK TOURS ONLINE
On-site prices are 30-50% higher. Book through trusted platforms and save 💰

3️⃣ BRING CASH
Many vendors don't accept cards. Have small bills (Egyptian pounds) ready for tips and purchases

4️⃣ DRESS COMFORTABLY
It's a LOT of walking on sand. Comfortable shoes + breathable clothes + sunscreen are essential

5️⃣ HAGGLE EVERYTHING
First price is never the real price. Start at 50% and negotiate with a smile 😊

Save this for your trip! 🔖

Full guide → Link in bio
```

## POST 7: Egyptian Food

### Caption:
```
Egyptian food is SO underrated 🍽️🇪🇬

Here's what you MUST try:

1️⃣ KOSHARI 🍝
Egypt's national dish! Pasta, rice, lentils, chickpeas, crispy onions + spicy tomato sauce. Carb heaven for $1-2!

2️⃣ FALAFEL (Ta'ameya) 🧆
Egyptian version uses fava beans. Crispy outside, fluffy inside. Best breakfast ever.

3️⃣ FUL MEDAMES
Slow-cooked fava beans with olive oil, lemon, cumin. Simple but SO good.

4️⃣ SHAWARMA 🌯
Juicy, flavorful, and available on every corner.

5️⃣ FRESH JUICE 🍹
Mango, sugarcane, guava... for less than $1!

6️⃣ OM ALI 🍮
Egyptian bread pudding with nuts, raisins, cream. Dessert perfection.

Pro tip: Eat where locals eat. Best food = tiny shops, not fancy restaurants!

What Egyptian dish are you most excited to try? 👇
```

## POST 8: Best Time to Visit

### Caption:
```
When's the BEST time to visit Egypt? 📅

Quick guide by season:

🌸 OCTOBER - APRIL (Peak Season)
✓ Perfect weather (20-25°C / 68-77°F)
✓ Comfortable for sightseeing
✓ Higher prices & more crowds
✓ BEST TIME for most travelers

☀️ MAY - SEPTEMBER (Off Season)
✓ HOT (35-45°C / 95-113°F)
✓ Fewer tourists
✓ Lower prices (30-40% off!)
✓ Best for: Red Sea beaches & diving

MY TOP PICKS:
🥇 October-November: Perfect weather + post-summer prices
🥈 February-March: Great weather + fewer crowds than Dec/Jan
🥉 December: Festive vibes but busiest time

PRO TIP: Avoid visiting during Ramadan if you want full restaurant access (dates change yearly)
```
