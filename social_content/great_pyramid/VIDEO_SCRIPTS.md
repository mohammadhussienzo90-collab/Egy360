# TECHNICAL NOTES FOR ALL VIDEOS

## Aspect Ratios:
- Instagram Reels: 9:16 (1080x1920)
- TikTok: 9:16 (1080x1920)
- YouTube Shorts: 9:16 (1080x1920)

## Text on Screen:
- Use bold, readable fonts
- White text with black outline/shadow
- Keep text on screen for 2-3 seconds minimum

## B-Roll Sources:
- Unsplash/Pexels (free)
- Stock footage sites (paid)
- Historical illustrations (public domain)
- Diagrams/animations (create in Canva)

## Audio:
- Background music: 20-30% volume
- Voice narration: Clear, engaging pace
- Sound effects: Subtle, enhance not distract

## Captions:
- Always include captions
- 85% of social video is watched on mute
- Use auto-captions, then correct errors

## Call-to-Action:
- Every video should have clear CTA
- "Follow for Part X"
- "Save this series"
- "Share with a history lover"
