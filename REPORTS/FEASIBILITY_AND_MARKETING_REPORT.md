## 1. MARKET ANALYSIS

### 1.1 Egypt Tourism Market Size

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 (Est.) | 2025 (Proj.) |
|--------|------|-------------|--------------|
| Tourist Arrivals | 14.9M | 15.5M | 17M+ |
| Tourism Revenue | $13.6B | $15B | $17B |
| Average Spend/Tourist | $912 | $968 | $1,000+ |

**Key Growth Drivers:**
- Grand Egyptian Museum opening (2024)
- New Sphinx Airport
- Red Sea resort expansion
- Government's 30M tourist target by 2028

### 1.2 Online Travel Booking Market

- **Global OTA Market:** $800B+ annually
- **Egypt Online Bookings:** ~35% of total (growing)
- **Mobile Bookings:** 60%+ of online travel
- **Affiliate Commission Rates:** 3-6% (hotels), 8-12% (tours)

## 9. CONCLUSION

### Feasibility Assessment: **VIABLE**

**Strengths:**
- Platform is fully built and functional
- Egypt tourism market is large and growing
- Low operational costs (< $200/month)
- Multiple revenue streams possible
- First-mover advantage in Egypt-focused niche

**Challenges:**
- Building organic traffic takes 6-12 months
- Affiliate revenue starts small
- Competition from global OTAs
- Need consistent content creation

**Recommendation:**
Proceed with launch. Focus on SEO and content marketing for the first 6 months while keeping costs minimal. Scale paid marketing only after proving organic acquisition model.

**Break-even Timeline:** 6-9 months
**Profitability Timeline:** 12-18 months
**Realistic Year 1 Revenue:** $1,500-3,000
**Realistic Year 3 Revenue:** $30,000-50,000
