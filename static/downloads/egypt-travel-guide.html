Day 1: Arrival in Cairo

Morning

Arrive at Cairo International Airport. Clear immigration and collect your visa on arrival ($25 USD). Take a taxi or Uber to your hotel in Giza or Downtown Cairo.

Afternoon

Rest and acclimatize. Take a short walk around your hotel area to get oriented.

Evening

Watch the Sound & Light Show at the Pyramids (7pm in winter, 8pm in summer). A magical introduction to ancient Egypt.

Pro Tip: Book your Sound & Light Show tickets online in advance at the official website.

Where to Stay

  • Budget: Meramees Hostel, Wake Up! Cairo ($15-25/night)
  • Mid-range: Pyramids View Inn, Steigenberger ($80-150/night)
  • Luxury: Marriott Mena House ($300+/night)

Day 2: Pyramids & Egyptian Museum

Morning (6am-12pm)

Great Pyramids of Giza - Arrive early to avoid crowds and heat. See the Great Pyramid, Pyramid of Khafre, Pyramid of Menkaure, and the Sphinx.

Tickets: Giza Plateau entry: EGP 200 (~$7). Great Pyramid interior: EGP 400 extra. Only 300 tickets/day - arrive by 7am!

Afternoon (2pm-6pm)

Egyptian Museum (Tahrir Square) - Home to King Tutankhamun's treasures, royal mummies, and 120,000+ artifacts.

Coming Soon: The Grand Egyptian Museum near the Pyramids opens in 2024 with even more spectacular displays!

Evening

Explore Khan el-Khalili bazaar. Get lost in the medieval maze of shops, enjoy mint tea at Fishawi's Cafe (open since 1773!).

Day 3: Cairo to Luxor

Morning

Optional: Visit Saqqara (Step Pyramid) and Memphis. About 2 hours round trip.

Travel Options to Luxor

Method Duration Cost
Flight 1 hour $80-150
Sleeper Train 10 hours (overnight) $80-100
Regular Train 9-10 hours $10-30
Recommended: Take the overnight sleeper train - saves a hotel night and arrives in the morning ready to explore!

Day 4: Luxor - East Bank

Morning

Karnak Temple Complex - The largest religious building ever constructed. Walk through the Avenue of Sphinxes, see the Great Hypostyle Hall with 134 massive columns.

Afternoon

Luxor Temple - Beautiful at sunset when the columns are illuminated. Connected to Karnak by the recently restored 2.7km Avenue of Sphinxes.

Evening

Walk the Luxor Corniche along the Nile. Enjoy a felucca sunset sail (~$10-15 for 1 hour).

Hot Air Balloon: Book for tomorrow's sunrise over the West Bank. $80-150/person. Absolutely magical!

Day 5: Luxor - West Bank

Sunrise (5am)

Hot air balloon ride over the West Bank (if booked). Unforgettable views of temples and the Nile at dawn.

Morning

Valley of the Kings - 63 royal tombs including Tutankhamun's. Your ticket includes 3 tombs. King Tut's tomb is extra (EGP 300).

Midday

Temple of Hatshepsut - Egypt's only female pharaoh built this stunning mortuary temple carved into cliffs.

Afternoon

Colossi of Memnon - Two massive statues of Pharaoh Amenhotep III. Free to visit!

Warning: The West Bank is HOT. Bring water, sunscreen, hat, and wear comfortable shoes. Start early!

Day 6: Luxor to Aswan

Option A: Train/Car (3-4 hours)

Take the morning train to Aswan. Stop at Kom Ombo Temple and Edfu Temple en route.

Option B: Nile Cruise (Recommended)

Join a 2-night Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan. Includes all meals, guided temple visits, and the most relaxing way to travel Egypt.

Evening in Aswan

Explore the Aswan Souk, take a felucca to Elephantine Island, enjoy dinner with Nile views at one of the floating restaurants.

Day 7: Aswan & Departure

Early Morning (Optional)

Abu Simbel Day Trip - Leave at 3am, return by noon. The relocated temples of Ramesses II are absolutely worth the early wake-up. ~$80-120 for minibus tour.

Morning

Philae Temple - Beautiful island temple dedicated to Isis. Take a boat across the lake.

Aswan High Dam - Engineering marvel that created Lake Nasser.

Afternoon

Nubian Village Visit - Experience colorful Nubian culture, try Nubian food, and meet the famously friendly locals.

Departure

Fly from Aswan to Cairo (1 hour) for your international connection, or extend your trip to the Red Sea!

Packing Checklist

Essentials

  • Passport (valid 6+ months)
  • $25 USD for visa
  • Copies of travel documents
  • Travel insurance
  • Credit/debit cards + cash

Clothing

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light, loose clothing
  • Modest dress for temples (shoulders & knees covered)
  • Warm layer for evenings/AC
  • Swimsuit (for hotel pools)

Sun Protection

  • Sunscreen SPF 50+
  • Sunglasses
  • Wide-brimmed hat
  • Reusable water bottle

Tech & Useful Items

  • Phone & charger
  • Power bank
  • Camera
  • Universal adapter (Type C)
  • Small flashlight (for tombs)

Essential Tips

Money: Egyptian Pound (EGP). 1 USD = ~30 EGP. ATMs widely available. Carry small bills for tips.
Bargaining: Expected everywhere except restaurants and supermarkets. Start at 30% of asking price.
Tipping (Baksheesh): EGP 10-20 for small services, EGP 50-100 for guides. Have small bills ready.
Scams to Avoid:
  • "The museum is closed" - It's not. They want to take you to a shop.
  • Unofficial guides at Pyramids - Use official guides only.
  • Free camel rides - Nothing is free. Agree on price BEFORE.
Best Time to Visit: October-April for pleasant weather (15-25°C). Avoid July-August (40°C+).
