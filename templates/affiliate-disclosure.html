Important Disclosure

Egy360 is a participant in various affiliate marketing programs. This means we may earn commissions when you click on links to partner websites and make a purchase or booking. This comes at no additional cost to you.

1. What is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a way for websites like Egy360 to earn a small commission by recommending products and services to our visitors. When you click on certain links on our website and complete a booking or purchase, we may receive a referral fee from the partner company.

Important: You will never pay more by using our affiliate links. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go directly to the partner's website.

3. How We Use Affiliate Links

On Egy360, you may encounter affiliate links in the following places:

  • Hotel and Accommodation Listings: "Book Now" or "Check Prices" buttons
  • Tour and Activity Pages: Links to book tours through partner platforms
  • Blog Posts and Guides: Recommendations within our travel content
  • Comparison Tables: Price comparisons linking to booking sites
  • Banner Advertisements: Display ads from partner programs

4. How Affiliate Links are Identified

We strive to be transparent about affiliate relationships. Affiliate links on our site may be identified by:

  • Text such as "Book via our partner" or "Affiliate link"
  • Buttons labeled with partner names (e.g., "Book on Booking.com")
  • Disclosure statements near link clusters
  • The rel="sponsored" attribute in the link HTML

5. Commission Structure

Commission rates vary by partner and product type:

  • Hotels: Typically 4-8% of the booking value
  • Tours & Activities: Typically 8-10% of the booking value
  • Travel Insurance: Varies by provider
  • Flights: Fixed fee per booking or small percentage

These commissions help us maintain and improve Egy360, creating more valuable content for travelers visiting Egypt.

6. Editorial Independence

Our editorial content is independent of our affiliate relationships:

  • We recommend products and services based on quality, not commission rates
  • Affiliate partnerships do not influence our reviews or recommendations
  • We include non-affiliate options when they provide better value
  • Negative reviews are published even for affiliate partners
  • Our travel guides are written to help you, not to maximize our earnings
Our Promise: We will never recommend a product or service solely because it pays us a commission. Our primary goal is to help you have an amazing trip to Egypt.

7. No Additional Cost to You

Using our affiliate links does NOT cost you anything extra. In fact:

  • Prices are the same as going directly to the partner site
  • Some partners offer exclusive discounts through our links
  • You still receive all normal benefits, rewards points, and guarantees
  • Your consumer rights are fully protected

8. Cookies and Tracking

When you click an affiliate link, a cookie may be placed on your device to track the referral. This cookie:

  • Allows the partner to credit us for the referral
  • May remain active for 24 hours to 30 days (varies by partner)
  • Does not contain personal information
  • Can be blocked through your browser settings

For more information, please see our Cookie Policy.
