Trip Planning Essentials

Everything you need to know before booking your Egypt trip

Ideal Duration

We recommend 7-14 days for a comprehensive Egypt experience.

  • 7 days: Cairo + Luxor highlights
  • 10 days: Add Aswan & Nile cruise
  • 14 days: Include Red Sea beaches

Getting There

Major airlines fly to Cairo International Airport (CAI).

  • Direct flights from major cities
  • Budget options via Istanbul, Dubai
  • Internal flights to Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada

Language & Currency

Arabic is official, English widely spoken in tourist areas.

  • Currency: Egyptian Pound (EGP)
  • USD/EUR accepted at hotels
  • ATMs available everywhere
