1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and understand how you interact with the site.

Cookies can be:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by Egy360 directly
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by our partners and service providers

2. How We Use Cookies

Egy360 uses cookies for the following purposes:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core features like user authentication, security, and session management.

Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve the user experience and content quality.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies track your activity across websites to deliver personalized advertisements and measure ad campaign effectiveness.

Affiliate Cookies

When you click on links to our travel partners (hotels, tours, flights), affiliate cookies are placed to track referrals. This allows our partners to credit us with a commission if you make a booking.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have control over how cookies are used on your device:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete all or specific cookies
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies
  • Set preferences for certain websites

Common browser cookie settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On
  • Digital Advertising: Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out
  • Network Advertising: Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out

Important Note

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the website to work properly. If you block all cookies, you may not be able to use all features of Egy360.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Currently, there is no universal standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals. Egy360 does not currently respond to DNT signals, but you can use the opt-out tools above to control tracking.
