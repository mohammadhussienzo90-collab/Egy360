Frequently Asked Questions

While not legally required, travel insurance is highly recommended. Medical care for tourists in Egypt can be expensive, and evacuation costs can reach tens of thousands of dollars. Insurance provides peace of mind for your trip.

Basic scuba diving (up to 30m) is covered under Standard and Premium plans. For technical diving or deeper dives, you may need additional coverage. Always check your policy details.

Coverage for pre-existing conditions varies by provider. Some offer waivers if you purchase within 14 days of your initial trip deposit. Disclose all conditions when purchasing to ensure proper coverage.

Some providers allow purchase after departure, but coverage may be limited. Trip cancellation coverage requires purchase before departure. It's best to buy insurance when you book your trip.
