Egypt Trip Planning Guide

Best Time to Visit Egypt

The ideal time to visit Egypt is from October to April, when temperatures are comfortable (20-25C). Summer months (June-August) can exceed 40C in Upper Egypt, though Red Sea resorts remain pleasant year-round. Ramadan dates shift annually and can affect opening hours.

How Many Days Do You Need in Egypt?

A minimum of 7-10 days is recommended to see Egypt's highlights: Cairo and the Pyramids (2-3 days), Luxor temples (2-3 days), Aswan and Abu Simbel (1-2 days), and optionally the Red Sea (2-3 days). A Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan takes 3-4 days. Our trip planner helps you optimize every day.

Egypt Travel Budget

Budget travelers can explore Egypt for $30-60/day including hostels, street food, and local transport. Mid-range travelers spend $80-150/day for 4-star hotels and guided tours. Luxury travelers enjoy 5-star experiences from $200-500/day including Nile cruises and private guides.

Getting Around Egypt

Domestic flights connect Cairo with Luxor (1h), Aswan (1.5h), and Hurghada (1h). Sleeper trains run overnight between Cairo and Luxor/Aswan. The Nile cruise between Luxor and Aswan is both transport and experience. For the Red Sea, buses from Cairo take 5-6 hours.

Travel Tips

  • Get your travel insurance before departure
  • Most nationalities get visa on arrival ($25 USD)
  • Book popular tours like Abu Simbel and hot air balloons in advance
  • Carry small bills for tipping (baksheesh is customary)
  • Download offline maps for areas with limited connectivity
