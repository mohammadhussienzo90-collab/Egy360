Tips for Booking Flights to Egypt

Best Time to Book

Book 2-3 months in advance for the best prices. Peak season is October-April, with higher prices during Christmas and Easter.

Main Airports

Cairo (CAI) is the main hub. Hurghada (HRG), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH), and Luxor (LXR) have direct international flights from Europe.

Save Money

Fly midweek (Tue-Thu) for lower prices. Consider flying into Cairo and out of Luxor/Hurghada to save time and money.

Egyptian International Airports

CAI

Cairo International

Egypt's busiest airport. Hub for EgyptAir. Gateway to pyramids and Nile cruise.
