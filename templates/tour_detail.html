About This Tour

Experience the wonder of ancient Egypt on this guided tour of the Pyramids of Giza and the Great Sphinx. Walk in the footsteps of pharaohs as you explore these magnificent structures that have stood for over 4,500 years.

Your expert guide will share fascinating stories and historical insights about how these incredible monuments were built and their significance in ancient Egyptian culture. You'll have plenty of time to take photos and marvel at these UNESCO World Heritage sites.
